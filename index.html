The course

Kallah Course Overview

Twelve lessons in four parts, from the meaning of marriage to the practical details of your first months as a married woman.

Part one

Foundations

  1. 01

    Your Course Plan

    Personal details, wedding timeline, family customs, rabbinic guidance, learning schedule, and reminders.

  2. 02

    The Purpose of Marriage

    The Jewish vision of partnership and creating a home where the physical and spiritual come together.

  3. 03

    Adam and Chava

    Creation, individuality, free choice, and the journey toward becoming one.

Part two

The Jewish Home

  1. 04

    Mitzvot and Meaning

    How mitzvot connect us to Hashem and elevate everyday life.

  2. 05

    The Three Mitzvot

    Challah, Shabbat candles, and family purity, with their practical foundations and deeper meaning.

  3. 06

    The Power of a Jewish Woman

    Your role in shaping the values, atmosphere, and spiritual direction of your home.

Part three

Family Purity

  1. 07

    Tumah and Taharah

    A clear introduction to spiritual transition, potential life, and renewal.

  2. 08

    Your Cycle

    Hefsek taharah, the seven clean days, bedikot, timing, stains, colors, and when to ask a question.

  3. 09

    Harchakot

    The practical guidelines for the niddah period at home and while traveling, including family customs.

Part four

Mikvah and Calendar

  1. 10

    Mikvah Preparation

    Chafifah, chatzitzot, immersion, prayer, appointments, and preparation for Shabbat and Yom Tov.

  2. 11

    The Jewish Calendar

    Tracking the cycle, calculating the required onot, using an app, and preparing for separation and mikvah.

  3. 12

    Personal Resources

    Customized dates, reminders, mikvah information, trusted contacts, helpful apps, prayers, and books.

Your teacher

Rachel Benchimol

Rachel Benchimol

Rachel Benchimol teaches kallahs with warmth, clarity and care. She created The Kallah Club so every bride can learn both the beauty and the practical details of a Jewish home, at her own pace, with a teacher she can turn to with any question.

Her goal for every kallah is simple: to walk to the chuppah feeling prepared, confident, and excited for the home she is about to build.
